The Journey

A Life Dedicated to Impact

Dr. Aniruddha Malpani is a rare polymath who has achieved excellence across multiple domains - from pioneering IVF treatments in India to becoming one of the country's most thoughtful angel investors.

Graduating from Bombay University with over 20 gold medals, including the prestigious Dorab Tata Special Merit Prize, Dr. Malpani's academic brilliance foreshadowed an extraordinary career. But numbers alone don't capture his essence - it's his unwavering commitment to ethics, transparency, and empowering others that defines him.

Whether he's helping an infertile couple achieve their dream of parenthood, mentoring a first-time founder, or speaking truth to power against corporate malpractice, Dr. Malpani brings the same integrity and passion to everything he does.

Speaking Truth to Power

Fearless Advocacy

Dr. Malpani believes in standing up for what's right, even when it's unpopular. His willingness to call out corporate malpractice and advocate for consumers has made him a respected voice in the Indian business ecosystem.

The Byju's Saga 2020

LinkedIn Ban for Exposing EdTech Malpractice

When Dr. Malpani learned that Byju's - then the world's most highly valued edtech startup - was using predatory sales tactics to pressure vulnerable parents, he spoke up on LinkedIn. His posts documented mis-selling practices, pressure tactics, and exploitative financing schemes.

Rather than address these concerns, Byju's had LinkedIn ban his account permanently - an account with years of thoughtful content. Dr. Malpani fought back, taking the matter to the Bombay High Court.

Vindicated: Byju's is now facing insolvency proceedings after defaulting on payments, validating Dr. Malpani's early warnings about their unsustainable practices.
